# Adult rice/corn-waffle replacement

Checked: 2026-09-16

## Recommendation

Replace the child-positioned Lilibiggs product with **Coop Reis Maiswaffeln Paprika, 80 g**.

- Adult-neutral Coop branding; the listing contains no child age claim or child-range positioning.
- It directly covers both parts of the example: **51% rice flour and 34% corn**.
- Coop sells it as one 80 g retail unit and currently exposes add-to-cart and store-availability controls.
- Ambient-vending compatible: Coop says to store it dry and cool, **not in the refrigerator**.
- Vending unit: dispense the unopened 80 g bag. The three 25 g portions are serving guidance, not separately verified sale units.

Product page: [Coop Reis Maiswaffeln Paprika, 80 g](https://www.coop.ch/de/lebensmittel/brot-backwaren/cracker-knaeckebrot/reiswaffeln/reis-maiswaffeln-paprika/p/4835222)
