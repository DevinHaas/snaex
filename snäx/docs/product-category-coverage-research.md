## Summary

The 25-slot catalog can cover all 15 requested examples with **nine one-for-one substitutions**, while keeping six existing products that already qualify. An unopened protein shake is feasible without refrigeration: **NUTRI ATHLETIC Plant Protein Drink is UHT and explicitly described as “uncooled shelf-stable.”** Fresh fruit is the only example that conflicts with the stricter “individually packaged ambient product” requirement; a whole apple is workable only after testing the machine’s delivery mechanism and setting a short inspection/rotation cycle. The proposed single-serve nut-butter pouch is vending-ready, but its exact unopened storage range should be obtained from the manufacturer before rollout.

## Procurement and rollout checks

1. **Protein shake:** approved in principle for an uncooled machine because two Swiss sales sources explicitly describe the sealed UHT product as shelf-stable without cooling. Record the printed maximum temperature and best-before date on receipt.
2. **Fresh apple:** this is fresh and ambient, but not individually packaged. If “packaged only” is absolute, no honest room-temperature substitute satisfies the exact “fresh fruit” example; refrigerated fruit cups are a different operating model. Test drop damage, use a gentle delivery mechanism and set a written replenishment/discard routine.
3. **Nut-butter pouch and pumpkin seeds:** get manufacturer/distributor storage specifications before purchase. Do not infer suitability from packaging alone.
4. **Multipack-derived units:** verify independent consumer labelling and barcodes before vending the individual iced-tea bottle.
5. **Packshots:** all selected products have usable images on the linked Swiss manufacturer/retailer pages. Confirm image-reuse rights or request official trade assets; availability of an online packshot is not itself a licence to copy it.

## Currency assessment and limitations

**The most recent sources found are live Swiss retailer/manufacturer pages accessed on 16 September 2026; most display no publication or update date.** Prices, stock and packaging can change quickly, so procurement should re-check the exact SKU, GTIN, pack dimensions and case quantity before ordering. Online listings substantiate current Swiss-market presence but do not guarantee wholesale supply. The only unresolved evidence gaps are the exact unopened storage range for Eric’s 45 g squeeze pouch and the Jucker seed pouch; the fresh-apple limitation is operational rather than nutritional.
