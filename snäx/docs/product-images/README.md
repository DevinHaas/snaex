## Selection and quality limits

Pixel dimensions describe the source canvas, not necessarily the product within it. A large white canvas does not make a narrow product higher resolution. Display limits below conservatively allow two source pixels per CSS pixel (2× screens); they are limits for the **entire image**, including its margins. Keep `object-fit: contain`. Do not crop packaging to fill cards.

## Rejected alternatives and remaining sourcing

- Brack's [Still case](https://assets.brack.ch/images2/6/2/9/295263926/295263926_xxl3.jpg) and [Prickelnd case](https://assets.brack.ch/images2/9/2/7/295263729/295263729_xxl3.jpg) are 3000 × 1888 px, but depict 24-packs and older branding. Rejected for single-vend cards despite their resolution; no copies retained.
- Coca-Cola HBC's older portfolio images are only 374 × 966 px and differ from current 2026 artwork. Retailer alternatives examined ranged from 600 × 600 to 325 × 1024, without establishing a better current exact-pack master. Do not enlarge the accepted 244 × 800 files to pretend they are high resolution.
- For **VALSER**, obtain current 500 ml PET masters, ideally at least 1600 px bottle height, with confirmation of which label generation will be supplied.
- For **Coop H4**, request article **7124958**, roasted/salted nut mix **50 g**, front-facing original with at least 1600 px product height; prefer transparent PNG, otherwise clean white background. Include permission for Snäx website/app product cards.
- For **DAR-VIDA**, confirm that the supplied 41.6 g Nature and 41.5 g Dinkel pocket wrappers match these artworks. If not, request those exact individually sold wrappers, not an outer retail pack. The Dinkel image must retain its genuine Coop branding.

These are ready-to-use sourcing specifications; no messages have been sent to suppliers. For the later feature, generate appropriately sized web derivatives from the originals when layout dimensions are known. Retain these originals and provenance; avoid shipping the multi-megabyte PNGs directly.
