## Selection caveats

- Product pages and CDN URLs are mutable and generally undated. The newest dated asset paths encountered are 2026 VALSER/Coca-Cola artwork; all links above were live or traceable on 14 September 2026.
- A high pixel count does not prove that the product itself fills the canvas. Use `object-fit: contain`, preserve the complete package, and inspect every downloaded original before publishing.
- The unresolved exact-unit risks are Valser Still, both Rivella bottles, Blévita Sesam, Farmer Soft Apfel and DAR-VIDA Nature. Their images are suitable representative assets, but physical-pack matching remains required.
- Rights remain unverified for every asset. Brand ownership and public delivery from a product page do not by themselves grant reuse permission.
